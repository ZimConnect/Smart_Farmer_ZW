Poultry Management ZW

Broilers (Ross 308, Cobb 500)
  • 0-14d: Starter 28% protein 32C brooding
  • 15-35d: Grower 24C
  • 35-56d: Finisher market 2kg
Layers (Hy-Line, Lohmann)
  • 0-8w starter, 8-18w grower, 18w+ layer 16% calcium
  • 16h light, 280-300 eggs/year
Roadrunner - Pfuyo yechibhoyi
  • Biggest killer Newcastle - vaccinate every 3 months!
  • Hay box brooder, free range + maize/sunflower
  • Market 12-16 weeks $8-12
